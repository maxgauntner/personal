Every security product promises visibility. But visibility without prioritization is just noise with better typography. Here's what I've learned designing triage tools for SOC teams.

## 1. Lead with the decision, not the data

Analysts aren't reading alerts — they're making a decision: *escalate, resolve, or investigate*. Design the screen around that decision.

## 2. Make the model explain itself

A risk score with no rationale gets ignored. A score that expands into its contributing signals becomes a tool.

## 3. Group by story

Ten alerts about one compromised account are one problem. Cluster them.

## 4. Respect the keyboard

Your power users live in their keyboards. `J`, `K`, and a command palette go a long way.

## 5. Measure time-to-confidence

Time-to-triage is useful. Time-to-*confidence* — how long until the analyst trusts their call — is better.
