AI is everywhere in security tooling now. The hard part isn't generating a summary — it's getting a skeptical analyst to believe it.

## Show your work

Every generated statement should link to the evidence it came from.

## Be honest about confidence

Calibrated language ("likely", "consistent with") beats false certainty.

## Keep the human in charge

Suggest, don't act — at least until the team has opted in.
